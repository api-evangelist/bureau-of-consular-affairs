# Bureau of Consular Affairs

> The Bureau of Consular Affairs (CA) is the bureau of the U.S. Department of State responsible for
> consular services: passports, visas, intercountry adoption, and travel advisories for every country
> in the world. It publishes no developer program and no API documentation of its own, but three real
> machine-readable surfaces are public and callable without a key: a CKAN open-data catalog, a set of
> ArcGIS Online Feature Services, and an RSS feed of travel advisories.
>
> Generated by API Evangelist from the artifacts in this repository. Not published by the bureau.
> Provenance: generated 2026-09-05, method generated, source apis.yml + repo artifacts.

## Read this first

- HTTP status codes lie on this provider. Both CKAN and ArcGIS return `200 OK` on failure and put the
  error in the response body (`success: false` / `error.code`). Branch on the body.
- Only three operations require a credential (dataset create/update/delete on the CKAN catalog).
  Everything else is anonymous.
- `cadatacatalog.state.gov` and `travel.state.gov` sit behind a Cloudflare WAF that blocks many
  automated clients outright with a 403 "Sorry, you have been blocked" page. The ArcGIS services and
  the `/_res/rss/` feed paths are not affected.

## APIs

- [CA Data Catalog (CKAN Action API)](https://cadatacatalog.state.gov/api/3/action): passport
  issuance statistics, visa data, adoption statistics and other consular datasets. Standard CKAN.
- [Travel Advisory Levels (ArcGIS Feature Service)](https://services6.arcgis.com/R6wlO6UHmSzqm9Vs/arcgis/rest/services/Travel_Advisory_Levels__(2024)_View_Layer/FeatureServer/54):
  one polygon per country carrying the current Level 1-4 advisory, its ISO 3166-1 alpha-3 code, the
  advisory date and the URL of the human advisory page.
- [Embassy and Consulate Locations (ArcGIS Feature Service)](https://services6.arcgis.com/R6wlO6UHmSzqm9Vs/arcgis/rest/services/Embassy_and_Consulate_Locations_View_Layer/FeatureServer/0):
  every U.S. post, with address, phone, emergency phone, email, and the post that provides consular
  services for the location.
- [Intercountry Adoption Statistics (ArcGIS Feature Services)](https://services6.arcgis.com/R6wlO6UHmSzqm9Vs/arcgis/rest/services/All_Years_Adoption_Statistics/FeatureServer):
  adoption counts, published as one service per year from 1999 plus all-years rollups.
- [Travel Advisories RSS](https://travel.state.gov/_res/rss/TAsTWs.xml): 216 dated items, each naming
  what changed in that advisory. The nearest thing the bureau publishes to a change feed.
